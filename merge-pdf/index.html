Merge PDF Online

ToolSarthi's PDF merger lets you combine multiple PDF files into one PDF document directly in your browser.

How to merge PDF files?

1. Select two or more PDF files.
2. Click the Merge PDF button.
3. Wait for processing to finish.
4. Download your merged PDF.

Is this PDF merger free?

Yes. ToolSarthi's PDF merger is free to use.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

The files are processed directly in your browser. They are not intentionally uploaded to our server.
